# Quest Map: what it is meant to be

Notes from Michael, 11 October 2026, for the rework. Not started yet.

The current page reads as an overview. It was meant to be two things at once:

1. **A working tool.** Someone with an idea should be able to use it to see exactly
   where to go next and why, step by step, including the doors that are closed to
   them and the reason. Example: why a first-time author cannot post to arXiv
   without an endorsement from someone already established there.
2. **A real look at modern science, mostly Western, as it actually runs.**
   - Graduate student burnout and the conditions behind it.
   - Where the money goes: billions for some lines of research, very little for others.
   - Extraction: who does the work and who keeps the value.
   - Most people in research are making a product of some kind. Foundational
     questions like the ones raised here are a small part of the field.

## Why it matters now

After incorporation, the institute intends to become a place others can affiliate
with for their grant process (an institutional home for independent researchers
who need one to apply). The map should show why that home is needed, and later,
how to use it.
