18-Month Curriculum Overview

The Elite Performance Training curriculum is structured in three progressive semesters, each building upon the previous foundation. All materials are open-source and available for download, modification, and replication.

Semester 1: Foundation

Months 1-6

Core Topics

  • Autonomic nervous system fundamentals
  • Diaphragmatic breathing techniques
  • Heart rate awareness and control
  • Progressive muscle relaxation
  • Heart rate variability optimization
  • Sleep and recovery protocols
  • Body scan and interoception
  • Stress recognition and management

Expected Outcomes

  • 5-8 bpm resting HR reduction
  • 20-25% HRV improvement
  • 15-20% athletic performance gains
  • 8-12% academic improvement

Semester 2: Optimization

Months 7-12

Core Topics

  • Mental rehearsal and visualization
  • Pre-competition protocols
  • Performance under pressure
  • Recovery optimization strategies
  • Injury prevention and body awareness
  • Test anxiety management
  • Sustained focus training
  • Memory and learning optimization

Expected Outcomes

  • 20-25% total athletic improvement
  • 12-15% academic improvement
  • 150-200 point SAT increase
  • Advanced autonomic control

Semester 3: Mastery

Months 13-18

Core Topics

  • Elite performance integration
  • College recruitment preparation
  • Advanced techniques (pain, temperature)
  • Teaching and peer mentoring
  • Independent practice mastery
  • Life skill integration
  • Long-term sustainability
  • Behavioral self-regulation mastery

Expected Outcomes

  • 25-30% total performance improvement
  • College admissions advantage
  • Self-directed mastery
  • Peer teaching capability

Implementation Guidelines

Getting Started

  1. Download the Complete Archive or select individual packages based on your needs
  2. Review the Master Curriculum Document to understand the full 18-month progression
  3. Train Instructors using the Level 1 Certification materials (minimum requirement)
  4. Set up Technology Infrastructure following the Technology Integration Guide
  5. Conduct Baseline Assessments using the Assessment Protocols package
  6. Launch Program with Week 1 materials and student practice guides
  7. Track Outcomes using monthly and quarterly assessment tools
  8. Share Results back with the research community (de-identified data)

Recommended Implementation Path

For institutions implementing this program:

  • 3-6 months before launch: Download materials, train instructors, set up technology
  • 2 months before: Conduct parent information sessions, recruit participants
  • 1 month before: Baseline assessments, equipment distribution
  • Launch: Begin with Week 1 Foundation curriculum
  • Ongoing: Weekly sessions, monthly assessments, quarterly reviews
