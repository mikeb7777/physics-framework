The process is the drama

Nothing needs to be dressed up. How an idea is questioned, funded, turned away or tested is a story in itself.

Learning, not winning

A prediction that holds, one that has to change and one that fails are all reported the same way: as what was learned.

People as they are

Careers, credit and conflict are part of science. We report what is documented, first-hand or public, and offer a reply.

The rules the series follows are set out in the Open Science Policy. Want to help test predictions yourself? Testing volunteers apply through the Members page.
