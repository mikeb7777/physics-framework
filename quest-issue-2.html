PeopleThe travellers

Each issue follows a small group of travellers, one or more on each lane of the map, through the same gates.

  • Real travellers have agreed to take part, have read what is said about them before it is published, and can reply in the next issue.
  • Composite travellers are not real people. Each is built from published figures for someone of that background on that lane, carries the same name in every issue, and is marked as a composite wherever it appears.
  • The figures come from the sources, not from the travellers. Five people cannot show how likely anything is; the travellers show what the published figures look like in a life.
