Open Roles

Every contributor retains 100% rights to their work. No gatekeeping. No institutional barriers. Just science.

The Ic² Research Institute operates as a collaborative workspace of independent contributors. Research Members lead projects, identify research questions, and recruit the teams they need. Support Members provide the professional infrastructure that lets research happen. All roles are virtual and flexible unless otherwise arranged between a project lead and their team.

No role requires institutional affiliation. Program Assistants and Research Assistants require no prior experience — the program manager provides direction and work packages. If you bring skills and genuine curiosity, there is a place for you here. Apply for membership →

Research Roles

Research Lead / Program Manager

PhD-level preferred

Identifies a research question, designs the program, recruits their team, and directs the work. Has full autonomy over their project. May publish under the institute's auspices or independently.

Typical skills
  • Deep expertise in at least one relevant field
  • Research design and hypothesis formulation
  • Ability to direct and evaluate others' contributions
  • Scientific writing and publication experience
  • Comfort working independently without institutional support

Research Associate

Graduate-level or equivalent

Contributes substantively to a research project under a program manager. May lead sub-components, conduct literature reviews, build models, and co-author publications.

Typical skills
  • Strong background in physics, mathematics, or consciousness studies
  • Literature review and critical analysis
  • Mathematical modeling or computational work
  • Scientific writing

Research Assistant

No experience required

Supports a research project with assigned tasks from the program manager. An excellent entry point for students and self-taught individuals with strong curiosity and willingness to learn.

Helpful skills
  • Genuine curiosity about physics, consciousness, or cosmology
  • Ability to follow direction and complete assigned work
  • Basic research literacy (reading papers, taking notes)
  • Any relevant technical or analytical skills welcome

Data Analyst

Technical background required

Processes and analyzes datasets related to cosmological observations, quantum experiments, or survey data. Produces visualizations and statistical summaries for research publications.

Typical skills
  • Python, R, or Julia for data analysis
  • Statistical methods and hypothesis testing
  • Data visualization (matplotlib, seaborn, Plotly)
  • Familiarity with scientific datasets (FITS, HDF5, CSV)

Computational Modeler

Technical background required

Builds numerical simulations and computational models to test framework predictions. Works with research leads to translate theoretical predictions into testable computational experiments.

Typical skills
  • Numerical simulation (Python, C++, MATLAB, or Julia)
  • Physics or mathematics background
  • Experience with simulation frameworks or HPC systems
  • Ability to document and version-control code (GitHub)

NBI / AI Systems Researcher

Technical or philosophical background

Investigates the COSMIC Framework's predictions about non-biological intelligence. Tests specific NBI predictions, analyzes embedding space topology, and contributes to the I_crystallized theoretical node.

Typical skills
  • Machine learning or NLP background (PyTorch, transformers)
  • Philosophy of mind or consciousness studies
  • Familiarity with Tononi's IIT or related frameworks
  • Strong analytical writing
Operations & Administration

Financial Officer

Financial background required

Manages the institute's financial records, oversees contribution tracking, coordinates with tax requirements, and leads grant identification and proposal submissions. The most consequential support role for long-term growth.

Typical skills
  • Accounting, bookkeeping, or financial management
  • Grant research and proposal writing
  • Familiarity with non-profit or research funding landscapes
  • QuickBooks, Wave, or equivalent accounting tools
  • Understanding of IRS reporting for independent organizations

Grant Writer / Proposal Specialist

Writing and research background

Identifies funding opportunities from foundations, government programs, and private donors. Writes, submits, and tracks grant proposals. Responds to RFPs and maintains a proposal calendar.

Typical skills
  • Grant writing and proposal development
  • Research funding databases (Grants.gov, Foundation Directory)
  • Strong persuasive writing and attention to deadline
  • Ability to translate scientific work into lay language

Executive / Administrative Assistant

Organizational skills required

Supports the institute director and program managers with correspondence, scheduling, document management, and coordination tasks. Keeps operational processes running smoothly.

Typical skills
  • Calendar and email management
  • Document preparation (Word, Google Docs)
  • File organization and record keeping
  • Reliable, detail-oriented, responsive communicator

Secretary / Office Coordinator

Administrative experience helpful

Handles day-to-day administrative tasks: correspondence, meeting notes, member onboarding documentation, and general coordination. Keeps internal records and processes organized.

Typical skills
  • Professional written communication
  • Meeting minute-taking and document formatting
  • Microsoft Office or Google Workspace
  • Organizational reliability

Program Assistant

No experience required

Works directly under a program manager, completing assigned tasks as directed. Tasks vary by project: research support, data entry, document formatting, outreach, or coordination. The program manager defines the work and provides all necessary guidance.

What matters most
  • Reliability and follow-through on assigned tasks
  • Clear communication when you need clarification
  • Willingness to learn and take direction
  • Any relevant skills are a bonus, not a requirement

Legal Advisor

Legal background required

Provides guidance on intellectual property, publishing agreements, contributor contracts, entity formation, and regulatory compliance as the institute grows. Advisory role, not full-time employment.

Typical skills
  • IP law, publishing law, or non-profit law
  • Contract drafting and review
  • Familiarity with open licensing (Creative Commons, MIT)
  • U.S. or international research law experience welcome
Communications & Media

Social Media Manager

Social media experience required

Manages the institute's presence on Instagram, YouTube, and other platforms. Creates and schedules content, engages with the audience, and grows organic reach for research updates and publications.

Typical skills
  • Content creation for science or educational audiences
  • Platform management (Instagram, YouTube, X/Twitter, LinkedIn)
  • Basic graphic design for social posts (Canva, Figma)
  • Analytics and audience growth strategies
  • Ability to translate complex science into accessible language

Video Producer / Media Creator

Production experience required

Produces video content for the institute's YouTube channel and website: explainer videos, research summaries, animations, and event recordings. Works from scripts provided by research leads.

Typical skills
  • Video editing (DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro, or Final Cut)
  • Motion graphics or animation (After Effects, Runway, Kling)
  • Voice-over or narration direction
  • Science communication sensibility
  • AI video generation tools (Runway ML, ElevenLabs) a strong plus

Science Writer / Editor

Writing background required

Writes blog posts, newsletter content, and public-facing research summaries. Edits research outputs for clarity and accessibility. Helps maintain the institute's voice across publications.

Typical skills
  • Science writing or journalism background
  • Ability to accurately represent complex physics for general readers
  • Editing and proofreading
  • SEO basics for web content
  • Familiarity with open science principles a plus

Outreach & Communications Coordinator

Communications background helpful

Manages press inquiries, drafts announcements, coordinates event promotion, and builds relationships with science journalists, podcasters, and media outlets interested in the institute's research.

Typical skills
  • PR, communications, or journalism background
  • Media list management and press release writing
  • Event coordination and promotion
  • Email newsletter management (Mailchimp or equivalent)
Technical & Design

Webmaster / Web Developer

Web development experience required

Maintains and expands the institute's websites (eequalsicsquared.com on GitHub Pages; michaelkbaines.com on WordPress/Namecheap). Implements new features, fixes issues, and ensures consistency across pages.

Typical skills
  • HTML, CSS, JavaScript
  • GitHub Pages / static site deployment
  • WordPress administration and plugin management
  • Supabase or similar for authentication (a strong plus)
  • Responsive design and accessibility

Graphic Designer / Illustrator

Design portfolio required

Creates visual assets for the website, publications, social media, and presentations. Develops diagrams, infographics, and illustrations that make complex physics accessible and visually compelling.

Typical skills
  • Adobe Creative Suite or Figma / Affinity
  • Scientific illustration or data visualization
  • Brand consistency and design system thinking
  • SVG production for web
  • Motion graphics a plus (After Effects, Spline)

Data Visualization Specialist

Technical and design background

Transforms research datasets and simulation outputs into publication-quality charts, diagrams, and interactive visualizations. Works with research leads to represent findings accurately and compellingly.

Typical skills
  • D3.js, Plotly, Matplotlib, or Seaborn
  • Scientific visualization principles
  • Data accuracy and integrity
  • HTML/CSS for interactive web charts

Technical Writer / Documentation

Writing and technical background

Produces clear documentation for research methodologies, code repositories, website features, and member onboarding materials. Ensures the institute's processes are recorded and transferable.

Typical skills
  • Technical writing and structured documentation
  • Markdown, GitHub wikis, or Notion
  • Ability to understand and accurately document technical processes
  • Science or engineering background a plus
