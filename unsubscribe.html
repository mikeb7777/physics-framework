Ic² Research Institute

The Ic² Research Institute is an independent platform advancing theoretical physics through open science, radical transparency, and community-driven inquiry. We develop falsifiable predictions across cosmology, quantum mechanics, and consciousness research — and we publish our failures alongside our successes.

Every contributor retains full ownership of their work. All findings are freely available to the global scientific community. No gatekeeping. No institutional barriers. Just honest pursuit of knowledge.
