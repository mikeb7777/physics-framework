Google Quantum AI published the Willow chip results confirming exponential error suppression per qubit layer. ALMA SPT2349-56 Results Published — Fourth Confirmed Prediction

ALMA observations of the SPT2349-56 protocluster confirmed intracluster gas at 5 times the expected temperature and star formation proceeding 5,000 times faster than standard models predict. This was the fourth independent experimental confirmation of a COSMIC Framework prediction, the second from cosmological structure formation.
