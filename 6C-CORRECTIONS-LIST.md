## B. Needs Michael's decision

### B1. The w₀ and wₐ values

Three places carry this (paras 1466, 1467, 3287). 6B currently says the values
w₀ ≈ −0.95 and wₐ ≈ −0.3 "do not match DESI's measured range and are under
review."

**"Under review" cannot go into print.** It is a holding phrase with no expiry,
and in a printed book it will still say "under review" in 2030. It needs to
resolve one way or the other. Two options, in section C below.

### B2. The parameter-free derivation, and whether it enters Element 8

The outstanding task is to derive the dark energy behavior from the framework
without fitting parameters. If that derivation exists before 6C goes to print,
it belongs in Element 8 and it changes the framework's standing: it would move
the dark energy claim from "direction only" to a real prediction with a number.

If it does not exist yet, **6C should not promise it.** A printed sentence
saying a derivation is forthcoming ages badly. Leave it out and let the website
carry the news when it lands.

## C. Recommended wording for B1

**Option 1, withdraw the numbers (recommended).**

> Earlier editions attached the values w₀ ≈ −0.95 and wₐ ≈ −0.3. Those values
> were not derived from the framework and do not fall within DESI's measured
> range. They are withdrawn. What the framework holds is directional: that dark
> energy evolves rather than staying constant. Turning that into a number
> requires a derivation from first principles, which is work in progress. The
> framework's real test is the prediction registered in advance for DESI's
> five-year analysis (COSMIC-005).

This is the conservative reading and the one that survives contact with a
critic. It costs a number and buys the right to be taken seriously.

**Option 2, keep them as historical, marked.**

> Earlier editions attached the values w₀ ≈ −0.95 and wₐ ≈ −0.3, which are
> recorded here for continuity. They were not derived from the framework and
> they fall outside DESI's measured range.

Weaker, because a reader skimming will take the numbers as the framework's
position regardless of the caveat.

## D. Print-only items, for the books.by edition

These do not apply to the PDF and have not been needed until now.

1. **ISBN.** The book has none. Check whether books.by supplies one, requires
   one, or sells without one. If a personal ISBN is used, it must be on the
   copyright page and it is specific to the print edition.
2. **Copyright line.** Currently `Copyright © 2025 by Michael Kevin Baines`.
   For a 2026 revised print edition the usual form is `© 2025, 2026`.
3. **The honor-system notice** (para 74) tells readers they may download and
   share the book. That is correct for the PDF. Decide whether a purchased
   printed copy should carry the same paragraph unchanged, since a buyer paid
   for it. Recommended: keep it, and add one line that the printed edition is
   sold at cost of printing and the text remains free online.
4. **A "this edition is a snapshot" line.** One sentence on the copyright page:
   the text is current as of its printing, the live version and every
   correction since are at eequalsicsquared.com/download.html. This protects
   every dated claim in the book at once, including the 2027 and 2028 test
   dates.
5. **QR code.** The security note about QR codes was written for downloaded
   copies. In a printed book bought from books.by, the code is trustworthy by
   definition, so the paragraph should say so rather than warning the reader
   away from the book in their hands.
6. **Formatting for the printer is Tina's**: trim size, margins, gutter,
   running heads, widow and orphan control, image resolution at print DPI, and
   whether the 456 pages need a different trim to bind well.
