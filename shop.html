The storefront is opening soon

We are setting up a shop so that printed editions of the book and the framework poster can be ordered directly, at cost, without going through a third party.

What will be available

  • Print editions of A Quest for The Big TOE
  • The COSMIC Framework poster, printed and shipped
  • Reference cards for the origin sequence and the open questions
  • Materials supporting the active research programmes

Everything the shop will sell is already free to download. The shop exists for people who would rather have a printed copy than make one, not to put anything behind a payment.

Available free, right now

No account, no payment, no email required

  • Download the book as a PDF — the current edition and its version history
  • Download the framework poster — print-ready PDF, 24 × 36 inches
  • View the poster in your browser
