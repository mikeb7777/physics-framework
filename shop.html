A look at what is coming: the mission medals

Every registered prediction becomes a mission, and every mission has a medal. It is struck for taking part, never for being right: the date on the rim is the day the prediction was deposited in public, before any data existed, and the back carries a spinner that turns in its bezel.

Midnight Eridanus

COSMIC-005. The dark energy prediction, waiting on DESI's five-year analysis. The real Eridanus stars, in their real positions.

Design finished · not manufactured

Midnight Orion

COSMIC-007. The isotropy prediction, waiting on the Rubin Observatory's first data release.
