Membership Structure

We welcome contributors at all levels, from PhD researchers leading projects to skilled professionals providing support and observers following our progress.

Research Members

PhD-level researchers who lead programs

  • Act as program managers and project leads
  • Recruit and manage project teams
  • Direct research initiatives
  • Retain 100% rights to work
  • Publish under institute or independently
  • Access collaborative resources

Support Members

Professionals providing essential services

  • Artists, designers, developers
  • Writers, editors, communicators
  • Administrative and secretarial support
  • Legal, accounting, operations
  • Work on assignments from leads
  • Virtual and flexible engagement
  • Retain full rights to creative work

Observers

Press, interested parties, and community

  • Read-only access to projects
  • Receive research updates
  • Invites to online meetings
  • Participate in open discussions
  • Complete transparency
  • Open to all site members
  • Includes press and media

How It Works

Research Members (PhDs) lead projects and recruit team members for their initiatives. They have full autonomy to assemble teams from Support Members and assign tasks as needed.

Support Members contribute their professional skills to research projects. While primarily virtual, leads may organize in-person collaborations for their teams.

Observers stay informed about all research activities, receive regular updates, and are invited to participate in public discussions and online meetings.

All members must sign up through the membership application process, regardless of their role or how they were recruited. This ensures transparency and proper coordination across all projects.
