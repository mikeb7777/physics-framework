The Quest newsletter

Every couple of months, a letter: what was predicted, what the instruments said, and how close the two came. Predictions are dated and public before the data that tests them exists, so nothing can quietly disappear and there is nothing to report but the accuracy. Readers are who that openness is for.

Who it is for
Anyone who wants to keep up with the research.
How often
Every two months, and a special issue when a prediction reports.
What it takes
An email address. No account, no approval.
Where it fits
A readership rather than a membership. Nothing is asked of you in return.

Member means something specific here

A member is someone working with the institute: they have applied, been approved, and hold an account in the member workspace. Subscribing to the newsletter is a different thing rather than a lesser one, and so is joining the forum. All three keep separate accounts, and signing up for one does not create the others.

We are strict about the word because membership carries a claim. Members appear on the Members page under their own names, and what they publish goes out with the institute's name on it.

If you want to follow what happens next, the newsletter is the right place to do it, and you can leave whenever you like.
