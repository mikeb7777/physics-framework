Thank You for Being Here

Whether you arrived through curiosity, a shared link, or your own research journey, we are grateful you found your way to the COSMIC Framework. Every reader, every question, and every idea shared strengthens the foundation of this work.

The Ic² Research Institute was founded on a simple conviction: scientific knowledge should be open to everyone. All research, predictions, and validation data are freely available because financial circumstances should never be a barrier to discovery.

Four independently validated predictions and 37 more under active testing. This framework is generating real results. Your support, in whatever form it takes, helps keep that work going.

A Thank You from Us

Free Download: COSMIC Framework Poster

Every contribution to open science matters, whether financial, intellectual, or simply showing up and engaging with the work. As a small expression of gratitude, this poster is a free download for anyone who visits this page.

The poster summarizes the COSMIC Framework's core predictions across cosmology, quantum mechanics, and consciousness science. Take it to any print shop and put it on your wall. It is a reminder that independent science, done openly and honestly, can hold its own against the full weight of experimental physics.

Print Shop Instructions

  • › File format: Download as PDF for best results. PNG at maximum resolution also works.
  • › Print size: 18 x 24 inches (standard poster) or 24 x 36 inches for a larger format. Both work well.
  • › Paper: Matte or semi-gloss photo paper, 80 lb or heavier. Matte produces the best result on the dark background.
  • › Color mode: Ask for CMYK printing if available. The print shop may need to convert from RGB, which is standard.
  • › Where to print: FedEx Office, Staples, Office Depot, UPS Store, or any local print shop that accepts PDF files.
