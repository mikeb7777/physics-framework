About This Reference List

Matched to Version 6E (October 2026). The numbers below are the numbers printed in the book. Each entry was checked against the sentence that cites it and against the publisher’s record, and each number marks one claim.

This page documents every source cited in A Quest for The Big TOE, the primary theoretical document of the Ic² Research Institute. The references are organized by the Element in which they appear and grouped by theme. Each section can be expanded independently.

The COSMIC Framework is a falsifiable theoretical proposal. These sources represent the existing scientific literature that the framework builds on, responds to, or makes predictions about. Where a source is available in open access, a direct link is provided. For paywalled journals, the DOI is linked so you can access through your institution or a legal preprint repository.

Pre-registered predictions and the institute’s own publications are archived at Zenodo, which dates each record and keeps the deposited files unchanged. Those records are not listed here but are referenced in the relevant Elements of the book.

On the reach of this list. We counted it in October 2026. Of 368 cited works, 96.5 percent were published in North America or Western Europe, and none is in a language other than English, German or Latin. That reflects where the author searched as much as where the research is done. We will repeat the count at every revision and publish the numbers. What we found, and what is changing, is in Reading the Whole Map; where to find research from every region is on the Global Research page.
